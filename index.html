Hoang Nguyen — Computer Engineering and Edge AI

Hoang Nguyen (Nguyen Le Minh Hoang) is a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), focused on edge AI, computer vision and multimodal retrieval. His work includes real-time vision pipelines, object detection and tracking, and deploying neural models on low-power edge accelerators.

Research and skills

His interests also include multimodal video and legal document retrieval, embedded systems, sensor fusion and smart IoT telemetry. His tools include Python, PyTorch, YOLO, CLIP, OpenCV, Milvus, BM25, dense embeddings, C and C++, and ESP32-S3. He is a research member of ACLab and the Understanding and Reasoning with AI research group at HCMUT.
