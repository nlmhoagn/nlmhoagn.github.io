About Hoang Nguyen

Hoang Nguyen, also known as Nguyen Le Minh Hoang, is a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City. His interests span edge AI, computer vision, multimodal retrieval and embedded systems.

His technical focus includes real-time vision pipelines, object detection and tracking, and deploying optimized neural models on low-power edge accelerators. He also works with multimodal video and legal document retrieval, vector databases, sensor fusion and IoT telemetry.

Hoang is a research member of ACLab and the Understanding and Reasoning with AI research group at HCMUT. His tools include Python, PyTorch, YOLO, CLIP, OpenCV, Milvus, BM25, C and C++, and ESP32-S3. For academic or research inquiries, see the contact page.
