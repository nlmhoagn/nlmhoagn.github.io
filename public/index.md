# Hoang Nguyen — Computer Engineering and Edge AI

Hoang Nguyen (Nguyen Le Minh Hoang) is a Computer Engineering undergraduate at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT), focused on edge AI, computer vision and multimodal retrieval. His interests include real-time vision, object detection and tracking, embedded systems, sensor fusion and intelligent IoT.

He is a research member of ACLab and the Understanding and Reasoning with AI research group at HCMUT. His tools include Python, PyTorch, YOLO, CLIP, OpenCV, Milvus, BM25, C and C++, and ESP32-S3.
